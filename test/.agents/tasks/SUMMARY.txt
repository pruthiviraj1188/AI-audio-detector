ROOT CAUSE
================================================================================

The prediction logic uses the wrong threshold:

CURRENT (BUGGY):
  threshold = 0.40  ← TOO LOW!
  if P(AI) > 0.40: predict as AI
  
  Problem: Human audio with P(AI) = 0.49 gets classified as AI
           This is exactly what users are reporting!

SHOULD BE:
  threshold = 0.50  ← Standard probability boundary
  if P(AI) > 0.50: predict as AI
  
  Result: Human audio with P(AI) = 0.49 correctly classified as Human

================================================================================
UNDERSTANDING THE RESULTS
================================================================================

What is P(AI)?
  - The model's probability that audio is AI-generated
  - Range: 0.0 (definitely human) to 1.0 (definitely AI)
  - Decision: if P(AI) > threshold → predict as AI

What is the threshold?
  - The decision boundary for binary classification
  - Standard value: 0.50 (50% probability = break point)
  - Current (buggy): 0.40 (too low, causes false positives)

Why threshold matters:
  - At 0.40: Audio with P(AI)=0.45 is classified as AI (WRONG)
  - At 0.50: Audio with P(AI)=0.45 is classified as Human (CORRECT)
  - At 0.40: Creates false positives (human audio → AI)

Why P(AI) is low for AI audio:
  - Model probabilities show mean P(AI) ≈ 0.39 for AI samples
  - This suggests feature extraction or model quality issues
  - But threshold adjustment still helps immediately
  - Model retraining recommended for long-term improvement
