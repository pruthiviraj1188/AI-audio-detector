STEP 2: UNDERSTAND THE BUG (1 minute)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CURRENT (WRONG):         SHOULD BE:                WHY:
────────────────────     ──────────────────        ──────────────────
threshold = 0.40         threshold = 0.50          Standard ML boundary
IF P(AI) > 0.40:         IF P(AI) > 0.50:         50% = uncertain
  → AI                     → AI                       Must pick a side

CONSEQUENCE:
Human audio with P(AI) = 0.49 is classified as AI (user's problem!)
